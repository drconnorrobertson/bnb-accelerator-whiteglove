Start the conversation ↗
What does BnB Accelerator actually do?+

We help you find and buy a short-term rental property and support the process with design and property-management introductions. We also connect you with independent CPAs and cost segregation providers to assess your tax strategy.

Do I own the property?+

Yes. The approach is centered on property that you purchase and own. Your consultation is an opportunity to discuss your goals, investment criteria, and the support available.

How hands-on will I need to be?+

Our aim is to reduce the work of sourcing and coordinating the purchase, design, and management. Ownership still involves decisions and responsibilities. Tax eligibility may also require your participation; discuss the requirements with your CPA.

Are cash flow and tax savings guaranteed?+

No. Results depend on the property, market, financing, expenses, operations, and your circumstances. Independent tax professionals must assess eligibility. Projections and past client experiences are not guarantees.

What happens during the consultation?+

You’ll discuss your investment goals, available capital, timeline, and questions. The team can explain the program, current fees, and next steps so you can determine whether the approach fits.

Can I work with my existing CPA?+

Yes. BnB Accelerator can make introductions to independent specialists, and you can continue working with your current advisor. BnB Accelerator does not provide tax or legal advice.
